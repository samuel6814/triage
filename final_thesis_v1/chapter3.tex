\chapter{METHODOLOGY}
\label{ch:method}

This chapter specifies the mathematical pipeline from an arbitrary chief complaint to a South African Triage Scale (SATS) colour and clinical pathway. Where concrete numbers aid understanding, we refer to a recurring illustrative example at \examplehospital{}: \runningcomplaint{} The same steps apply to any valid clinical text that passes the relevance gate in Section~\ref{sec:gate}.

The chatbot supports triage staff; it does not diagnose disease or replace clinical judgment.
