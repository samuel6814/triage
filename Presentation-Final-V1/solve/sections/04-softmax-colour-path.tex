\section{Step 5 --- Softmax classification (thesis numbers)}
\label{sec:softmax}

The five-class head:
\[
  \mathbf{z} = W h_{\mathrm{[CLS]}} + \mathbf{b},
  \qquad
  \hat{y}_c = \frac{\exp(z_c)}{\sum_{j=1}^{5}\exp(z_j)}.
\]

\subsection*{Illustrative logits (from the thesis example)}

After the linear map, suppose
\[
  \mathbf{z} = [-1.2,\,-0.5,\,1.8,\,0.3,\,-0.9].
\]

Compute exponentials:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}crr@{}}
\toprule
Level \(c\) & \(z_c\) & \(\exp(z_c)\) (approx.) \\
\midrule
1 (Red) & \(-1.2\) & \(0.301\) \\
2 (Orange) & \(-0.5\) & \(0.607\) \\
3 (Yellow) & \(1.8\) & \(6.050\) \\
4 & \(0.3\) & \(1.350\) \\
5 & \(-0.9\) & \(0.407\) \\
\midrule
Sum & & \(8.715\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection*{Probabilities}

\[
\begin{aligned}
  \hat{y}_1 &= 0.301/8.715 \approx 0.035, \\
  \hat{y}_2 &= 0.607/8.715 \approx 0.070, \\
  \hat{y}_3 &= 6.050/8.715 \approx 0.694, \\
  \hat{y}_4 &= 1.350/8.715 \approx 0.155, \\
  \hat{y}_5 &= 0.407/8.715 \approx 0.047.
\end{aligned}
\]

Check: \(0.035+0.070+0.694+0.155+0.047 \approx 1.00\).

\subsection*{Chosen level}

Largest probability is \(\hat{y}_3 \approx 0.69\) (about \(72\%\) in rounded deployment talk).

\[
  \hat{c} = 3.
\]

\begin{resultbox}
For this complaint we take \textbf{acuity level 3}.
\end{resultbox}

\begin{saybox}
``Softmax gives five beliefs that sum to one; we pick the largest---here level 3.''
\end{saybox}

\section{Step 6 --- Colour map \(f_{\mathrm{SATS}}\)}
\label{sec:colour}

\[
  f_{\mathrm{SATS}}(\hat{c}) =
  \begin{cases}
    \mathrm{Red} & \hat{c}=1 \\
    \mathrm{Orange} & \hat{c}=2 \\
    \mathrm{Yellow} & \hat{c}=3 \\
    \mathrm{Green} & \hat{c}\in\{4,5\}
  \end{cases}
\]

With \(\hat{c}=3\):
\[
  C_{\mathrm{NLP}} = f_{\mathrm{SATS}}(3) = \mathbf{Yellow}.
\]

\begin{resultbox}
\textbf{Colour for our complaint: Yellow.}
\end{resultbox}

\begin{stepbox}
Integer level \(\to\) hospital colour language.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{saybox}
``Level three maps to Yellow under \(f_{\mathrm{SATS}}.\)''
\end{saybox}

\section{Step 8 --- Full solution recap}
\label{sec:recap}

\begin{resultbox}[Solved: \runningcomplaint{}]
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.35em]
  \item Gate: \(g(X)=0.75 \ge 0.35\) \(\to\) proceed.
  \item Tokenise: WordPiece IDs with \texttt{[CLS]}/\texttt{[SEP]}/pad to \(128\).
  \item Embed: word+pos+seg \(\to H^{(0)}\) (768-d in the real model).
  \item BioBERT: 12 layers of attention+FFN \(\to h_{\mathrm{[CLS]}}\).
  \item Softmax: \(\hat{\mathbf{y}}\approx(0.03,0.07,0.69,0.16,0.05)\), \(\hat{c}=3\).
  \item Colour: \(C_{\mathrm{NLP}}=\mathrm{Yellow}\).
  \item Pathway: \(P(\mathrm{Yellow})\) with \(\approx 60\)\,min urgent stream at \examplehospital{}.
\end{enumerate}
\[
  X \xrightarrow{g} \cdots \to \hat{c}=3
  \xrightarrow{f_{\mathrm{SATS}}} \mathrm{Yellow}
  \to P(\mathrm{Yellow}).
\]
\end{resultbox}
