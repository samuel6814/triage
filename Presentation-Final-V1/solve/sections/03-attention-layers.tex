\section{Step 4 --- Attention (illustrative) and 12 layers}
\label{sec:attn}

In the real model, each layer builds
\[
  Q = H W_Q,\quad K = H W_K,\quad V = H W_V,
\]
then
\[
  \mathrm{Attention}(Q,K,V)
  = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^{\top}}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V,
  \qquad d_k = 64
\]
(with 12 heads so \(12\times 64 = 768\)).

We cannot multiply the true \(W_Q\) here. Instead we \textbf{solve a tiny attention problem} with \(d_k=2\) so every number is visible.

\subsection*{Toy setup: \texttt{[CLS]} listening to three keys}

Suppose the query for \texttt{[CLS]} and three keys (symptom-like / filler) are:

\begin{align*}
  q &= [1.0,\, 2.0], \\
  k_1 &= [1.0,\, 0.0] \quad\text{(``head''-like)}, \\
  k_2 &= [0.0,\, 1.0] \quad\text{(``fever''-like)}, \\
  k_3 &= [0.2,\, 0.1] \quad\text{(``I''-like filler)}, \\
  v_1 &= [1,\, 0],\quad
  v_2 = [0,\, 1],\quad
  v_3 = [0,\, 0].
\end{align*}

\subsection*{Compute scores \(e_j = (q\cdot k_j)/\sqrt{d_k}\)}

Here \(\sqrt{d_k}=\sqrt{2}\approx 1.414\).

\begin{align*}
  q\cdot k_1 &= 1.0,\quad
  e_1 = 1.0/1.414 \approx 0.707, \\
  q\cdot k_2 &= 2.0,\quad
  e_2 = 2.0/1.414 \approx 1.414, \\
  q\cdot k_3 &= 0.4,\quad
  e_3 = 0.4/1.414 \approx 0.283.
\end{align*}

\subsection*{Softmax weights \(\alpha_j\)}

\begin{align*}
  \exp(e_1) &\approx 2.028,\quad
  \exp(e_2) \approx 4.114,\quad
  \exp(e_3) \approx 1.327, \\
  \sum\exp(e_j) &\approx 7.469, \\
  \alpha_1 &\approx 0.271,\quad
  \alpha_2 \approx 0.551,\quad
  \alpha_3 \approx 0.178.
\end{align*}

Check: \(0.271+0.551+0.178 = 1\).

\texttt{[CLS]} listens most to the ``fever''-like key (\(\alpha_2\approx 0.55\)), then ``head'', then filler.

\subsection*{Mix values}

\[
  \mathrm{output}
  = \alpha_1 v_1 + \alpha_2 v_2 + \alpha_3 v_3
  \approx 0.271\,[1,0] + 0.551\,[0,1] + 0.178\,[0,0]
  = [0.271,\, 0.551].
\]

\begin{stepbox}
Scores \(\to\) weights that sum to 1 \(\to\) weighted mix of value vectors.
That is one attention head on a toy. BioBERT does this with \(d_k=64\), 12 heads, inside each of 12 layers.
\end{stepbox}

\subsection*{After 12 real layers}

\[
  H^{(0)} \to H^{(1)} \to \cdots \to H^{(12)}.
\]
Take the first row:
\[
  h_{\mathrm{[CLS]}} = H^{(12)}_{0,:} \in \mathbb{R}^{768}.
\]

\begin{notebox}
We do not write out the true \(768\) numbers. For the next step we only need:
``\(h_{\mathrm{[CLS]}}\) is a fixed 768-vector summary of our complaint.''
\end{notebox}

\begin{saybox}
``Attention: match query to keys, scale, softmax, mix values. Twelve layers later, read \texttt{[CLS]}.''
\end{saybox}
