\section{Step 1 --- Clinical gate}
\label{sec:gate}

Before BioBERT runs, score whether \(X\) looks clinical.

\[
  g(X) = \mathrm{clip}\!\Bigl(\sum_j s_j(X) - p(X),\, 0,\, 1\Bigr).
\]
Proceed only if \(g(X) \ge 0.35\).

\subsection*{Scores for our complaint}

Symptom lexicon hits (illustrative, matching the thesis style):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lc@{}}
\toprule
Hit & Score \(s_j\) \\
\midrule
headache & \(0.30\) \\
feverish & \(0.25\) \\
aches / weak (body) & \(0.20\) \\
\midrule
\(\sum_j s_j\) & \(0.75\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Greeting / non-clinical penalty: \(p(X)=0\) (this is a real complaint, not ``hello'').

\[
  g(X) = \mathrm{clip}(0.75 - 0,\, 0,\, 1) = 0.75 \ge 0.35.
\]

\begin{resultbox}
\textbf{Gate decision:} PASS. Continue to tokenisation.\\
(If this had been ``Hello, how are you?'' with \(p(X)=0.5\) and no symptoms, \(g=0\) and we would stop.)
\end{resultbox}

\begin{stepbox}
We did not assign a colour yet. We only decided the text is clinical enough for BioBERT.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{saybox}
``Gate score \(0.75\) beats the \(0.35\) threshold, so we proceed.''
\end{saybox}
