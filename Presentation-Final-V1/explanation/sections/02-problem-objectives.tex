\section{Problem statement, research gap, and objectives}
\label{sec:problem}

\subsection{Problem statement (slide 8)}

\begin{whybox}
States the pain in two blocks: lost urgency for Red/Orange patients, and Green congestion blocking capacity.
\end{whybox}

\paragraph{Lost urgency.}
Critical patients can lose time-critical priority when they move between imaging, labs, and wards.
There is often no digital record that carries colour and a countdown timer with them.

\paragraph{Green congestion.}
Non-urgent patients lack automated guidance to outpatient or self-care routes.
They occupy space and staff needed for life-saving care.

\begin{saybox}
``Two failures: urgent patients lose their colour as they move, and non-urgent patients clog the stream. We want a digital colour and pathway that travels with the patient.''
\end{saybox}

\subsection{Research gap (slide 9)}

\begin{whybox}
This is the academic justification. Examiners listen carefully here.
\end{whybox}

The gap has three layers:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Formal mathematics from patient text \(\to\) SATS colour \(\to\) local pathway for Ghanaian intake is under-specified.
  \item Most NLP triage papers use high-income corpora; local department routing is rarely formalised.
  \item TEWS covers vitals; the text-first moment lacks an examinable pipeline.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What this thesis fills.}
\[
  X \;\to\; \hat{\mathbf{y}} \;\to\; C_{\mathrm{NLP}} \;\to\; P(C)
\]
at \examplehospital{}, for English chief complaints.

\begin{saybox}
``We fill the gap with an end-to-end, examinable chain: English complaint to probabilities, colour, and pathway card.''
\end{saybox}

\subsection{Objectives (slide 10)}

\begin{whybox}
Maps the thesis aims to what the chatbot does.
\end{whybox}

\textbf{General objective:} develop a hospital chatbot that classifies unstructured English chief complaints into SATS colours and clinical pathways at intake.

\textbf{Specific objectives (remember the four):}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Automate triage classification from a single chief complaint.
  \item Guide clinical pathways (urgent vs routine streams).
  \item Integrate with SATS and \examplehospital{} routing.
  \item Support refinement via visit-informed pathway design.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{saybox}
``Our aim is one sentence: from one English chief complaint, produce a colour and a pathway that matches SATS-style routing at KNUST University Hospital.''
\end{saybox}
